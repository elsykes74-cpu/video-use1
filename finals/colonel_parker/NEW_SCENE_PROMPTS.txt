PART A - REPLACEMENT FOR THE "MOVIE ELVIS" PROBLEM
==========================================================================

The current young-Elvis frame reads as a modern actor playing Elvis rather
than Elvis. That happens because generators default to the most photographed
recent depiction. Fix: describe a PERIOD PHOTOGRAPH, not a person - film
stock, era, and camera do more work than the face description.
